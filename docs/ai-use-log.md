The log reflects the AI uses disclosed by the team during the project, including the additional Android implementation assistance documented above.

The architecture diagram and documentation describe the planned architecture and do not imply that every listed class or operation is already implemented.

AI-assisted implementation and documentation must be reviewed and understood by the team members. Team members remain responsible for verifying the accuracy of the submitted work, testing the application, and explaining the design and implementation decisions.
